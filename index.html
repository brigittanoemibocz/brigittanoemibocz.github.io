Background

My project experience comes from the Nyugat-Dunántúli Vízügyi Igazgatóság (West Transdanubian Water Directorate) in Hungary, working on domestic and cross-border EU-funded projects.

  • Project leadership. As project manager on the lead-partner side, I directly led three Interreg projects: ForMURA (Hungary–Croatia), AquaPinka and RaabSTAT (Hungary–Austria), with a combined total budget of approximately €1.44 million. I coordinated the work of technical, finance and procurement colleagues and prepared and chaired steering committee meetings.
  • Digital development. I led ForMURA, which enhanced a flood forecasting system, from preparation to closure, and coordinated the partner side of Raab Flood 4Cast. In both projects, I was responsible for communicating the EU funding programme’s content and documentation requirements, checking compliance, and clarifying these requirements with developers. I also participated in technical coordination meetings.
  • Portfolio follow-up. I tracked schedules, budgets and risks across 6–9 concurrent projects and prepared management status reports.
  • Earlier. I led a five-person project coordination team, and from 2009 to 2018 I was responsible for a France–Hungary wetland twinning programme between Kis-Balaton and Lac de Grand-Lieu.

I earned the PMP® certification in 2025 and an Agile project management certification in 2026, which the Agile-Vet case study documents.
